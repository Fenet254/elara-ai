She became more comfortable tracing a system line by line rather than making random changes until something appeared to work.

# Problem-Solving Mindset

Fenet tends to approach technical problems by first trying to understand what is actually failing.

When something does not work, she increasingly tries to avoid immediately changing multiple things at once.

Instead, she prefers to:

1. reproduce the problem,
2. inspect the error,
3. identify where the failure occurs,
4. trace the relevant code,
5. understand the assumptions,
6. isolate the problematic component,
7. test a hypothesis,
8. make a targeted change,
9. verify the result.

Her debugging philosophy has been strongly influenced by her internship experience.

The line-by-line debugging approach taught by her mentor at INSA became particularly influential.

