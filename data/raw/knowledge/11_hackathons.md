# Hackathons

## Gebeta Voice — AI x E-Commerce Hackathon
Focused on reimagining e-commerce with AI. Built "Gebeta Voice," an AI-powered 
solution enabling illiterate Ethiopians to access e-commerce using voice commands 
in local languages, aimed at making digital commerce more inclusive and accessible. 
Hosted at Jimma University Innovation Incubation Center.

## Fayda National ID Hackathon
A two-phase national competition. Teamed with Fenet Kenenisa and Sena Sura to 
build a Fayda ID–based Digital Queue & Token System — a solution for improving 
public service access through secure digital queues, real-time updates, and 
user-friendly authentication. The team advanced to the Virtual Phase of the 
competition (National ID Program, July 25 – August 4, 2025).

## Reboot the Earth 2025 — Addis Ababa Edition
Powered by FAO (Food and Agriculture Organization), in collaboration with MInT 
and EYEA. Out of 198 teams that applied, our team "Green Guardians" (with Ebisa 
Berhanu, Yonas Abebe, Solomon Tesfaye, and Anam Tesfa) was selected as one of the 
12 finalists. Over two intense days (August 9–10, 2025) at Addis Ababa Science & 
Technology University, we worked on tackling climate challenges through the 
combination of AI and agriculture.
